# Overwave

Native internet television for macOS. Browse a worldwide channel catalogue, watch in the integrated viewer or floating TV window, and organize channels with favorites and folders.

## Download

[Download the latest Overwave installer](https://github.com/markdelfs/teleliner-releases/releases/latest)

Requires macOS 14 or later. Universal build for Apple silicon and Intel Macs. Open the DMG and drag Overwave into Applications.

## Features

- Integrated live video viewer and compact channel table.
- Worldwide iptv-org catalogue, search, and country/category/language filters.
- Favorites, channel folders, and watch history.
- M3U playlist import from a file or URL, with category filtering and clear pagination.
- Broadcast channel identifiers and playback controls that hide after inactivity.
- Experimental Google Cast via a Chrome controller; receiver playback depends on your device and stream.
- Full screen, AirPlay, and an app-owned floating TV window.
- Split View for two channels with independent playback and one audible channel at a time.
- Sparkle update checks and automatic update preferences.

Streams may be unavailable or region restricted. Playback supports formats and codecs provided by macOS AVFoundation. Programme guides and recording are not included.

## Updates

Overwave was previously named Teleliner. The existing repository address and app identity are retained so installed copies continue receiving updates and keep their saved library.

The app uses this repository’s signed appcast for Sparkle updates. Release downloads are Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple.
